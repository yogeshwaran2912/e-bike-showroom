Frequently Asked Questions

What is the range of a solid-state battery?

Our flagship Model S gets up to 180 km on a single charge in Eco mode, while the compact City commute Model C yields 120 km. Range changes depending on terrain gradients, rider weight distribution, wind drag, and which riding modes are engaged.

How does Warp charging technology work?

Warp charging feeds high-current power directly to the cells. Our smart OS communicates continuously with the charge point to dial cooling systems and control current, enabling 0-80% power recovery in just 90 minutes safely without heating cells.

Do I need a special motorcycle driver's license?

Motorcycles like the Model S and Model A require a valid standard motorcycle license. However, urban commuters like the Scooter V and Model C may fall under light electric vehicle classifications in specific states, requiring only basic driver permits.
