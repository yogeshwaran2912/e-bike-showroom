Frequently Asked Questions

What brands are available at AV Motors?

We are the authorised dealers for India's leading EV manufacturers: iVOOMi, Battre, ONZO, and BMR. We also retail premium models like the Partner Pro Plus and custom loaders.

What is the discount policy during the AV Aadi Sale?

Under the AV AADI SALE, we offer a flat ₹10,000 discount on all electric bike purchases, along with Zero Down Payment options, 1 year of free servicing, and a lucky draw entry to win a brand new LED TV or a Gold Coin!

Do you offer loaders and industrial electric vehicles?

Yes, we offer heavy-duty electric commercial loaders including the Sumo Loader, BMR HL 200, and our specialized Three Wheeler Loader designed for business freight transit.

What are the battery specifications and warranties?

Most of our lineup features IP67-rated water-resistant Lithium-ion and LFP battery modules, providing ranges up to 130 km per charge. Manufacturer warranties range from 3 to 5 years depending on the model selected.
